# Discovery response playbook

Founder playbook for inbound form/email leads. Goal: reply within one business day, qualify fast, protect time.

---

## 1. When a lead arrives

Source: website form → email, or direct email to ogumaeluan@gmail.com.

**In the first 24 hours**
1. Read the problem description once without drafting.
2. Tag mentally: **fit / maybe / not now**.
3. Reply using a template below (personalize 2–3 lines).

---

## 2. Fit criteria (yes)

- Clear decision or operational pain (not “we want AI” only)
- Someone who owns the problem (or can introduce the owner)
- Data or process exists or can be accessed in a defined window
- Budget conversation possible after scope (not pure student/hobby unless strategic)

**Maybe:** interesting problem, unclear owner or data — ask two clarifying questions.  
**Not now:** pure staff-aug commodity coding, guaranteed ROI theater, or scope we cannot staff.

---

## 3. Reply templates

### A. Strong fit — invite discovery call

Subject: Re: [their topic] — discovery call

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out. [One sentence that shows you understood their problem].

I work on decision systems and production ML for ops and technology teams — with validation gates and clear ownership before anything goes live.

A short discovery call is free and no obligation. On the call we would clarify:
- the decision and cost of being wrong
- data access and constraints
- what “done” looks like

Are you available [two options in their timezone], or send a time that works?

Best,  
[Your name]  
Elite-Data-Intelligence

---

### B. Needs clarification

Subject: Re: [their topic] — two questions

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the note. To see whether I can help, two questions:

1. Who owns the decision or process today?  
2. What data (systems, history, quality) can we access in the first two weeks?

Once I have that, I’ll suggest a next step or a short call.

Best,  
[Your name]

---

### C. Not a fit (polite close)

Subject: Re: [their topic]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for writing. From what you described, [honest reason — e.g. pure implementation staffing / consumer app build / outside current focus]. That is not where Elite-Data-Intelligence is strongest today.

[Optional: one referral or resource if you have one.]

Happy to revisit if the problem shifts toward decision systems or production ML workflows.

Best,  
[Your name]

---

## 4. After a discovery call

1. Same day: short thank-you email with 3 bullets (decision, metrics, open risks).  
2. Within 2–3 business days: proposal/SOW from `docs/commercial/proposal-sow-template.md` or a one-page options note.  
3. Log the lead privately (date, fit, next step). Do not put confidential client detail in the public repo.

---

## 5. What not to promise on the first call

- Guaranteed KPI uplift without a scoped metric and data  
- EU AI Act certification or legal advice  
- Fixed price before decision and data constraints are known  
- That all 16 agent roles run as unsupervised LLM workers

Point to the public flagship project and operating model when they ask for proof of process.
