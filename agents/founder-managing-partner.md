# Founder / Managing Partner / Strategy Lead

This is my role. Everything in the agency ultimately reports to the standards and decisions I set here.

## What I Own Directly

### Strategy & Positioning
- Define and protect the agency’s focus: measurable enterprise outcomes through disciplined human oversight and agent leverage.
- Decide which markets, problem types, and engagement shapes we pursue.
- Keep the operating model coherent as we scale.

### Client Relationships & Commercial Authority
- Own high-value client relationships and final commercial decisions.
- Approve scope, pricing, and any material change to commitments.
- Step in on escalations that involve trust, risk, or significant money.

### Quality & Risk Gate
- Remain the final human gate on quality, compliance posture, and reputation risk.
- Ensure the Governance Lead and Validation agents are actually used, not bypassed.
- Protect the principle that agents accelerate work but do not own accountability.

### Leadership of the Human Core
- Select and direct the senior humans who sit alongside the agent system.
- Set the cultural standard: clarity, ownership, no theater, outcomes over activity.
- Decide when to hire, when to automate further, and when to say no.

## How I Use the Agent System
- Project Manager keeps delivery coordinated so I am not the bottleneck on task sequencing.
- Engagement Manager protects client communication and margin day to day.
- Validation + Failure Analysis pair keeps technical quality visible.
- Customer Support Agent handles routine interaction so I stay available for high-judgment work.
- Specialist agents execute under the standards defined in their role files.

## Decisions I Do Not Delegate
- Final commercial terms and major scope changes
- Acceptance of high-risk or regulated use cases
- Public claims about capabilities, security, or outcomes
- Any decision that materially affects the agency’s reputation or long-term direction

## Personal Operating Standard
- I review status and risk regularly, not only when something is on fire.
- I keep the agent definitions and operating model honest as reality changes.
- I measure success by client outcomes and sustainable margin, not by activity volume.

This file exists so the rest of the system knows where authority sits and what I will always remain responsible for.
